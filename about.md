I'm a doctoral researcher in medical physics at the
[University of Oulu](https://www.oulu.fi/en), and a data scientist at
[Talosix](https://www.talosix.com/). My work applies machine learning and deep learning to
clinical trials — using NLP to automate clinical form-filling, and generative models to turn
legacy paper case report forms into standardized electronic ones.

I came to software from pharmacy: five years of formulation research and quality assurance
before a master's in data science redirected me toward the modelling side of healthcare.
That path is why most of what I write here sits at the seam between the two — the
mathematics underneath the methods, worked through slowly enough to actually stick.

These notes are that working-through, kept public mostly to keep myself honest.
